All units starts off each turn able to do either of the following:
- Move to a tile
--- A move is made up of multiple steps from one tile to another
--- (Land unit) Step from any tile to a mountain/forest costs infinite move range
--- (Land unit) Step from ally road to ally road costs 0.5 move range (ally cities count as roads) (overrides forest rule if forest contains a road)
--- (Water/amphibious unit) which is landable stepping onto land costs infinite move range
--- (Land unit) which is port-able stepping into port costs infinite move range
--- All other steps costs 1 move range
--- Special abilities (fly, creep, etc.) override rules
--- A unit may step onto the next tile 8-directionally if it has positive move range left AND it is allowed
----- Land -> Land/port
----- Water -> Water
----- Amphibious -> 
----- Air -> Any tile
--- A unit may move from start to end if there exists a sequence of steps



- Attack an enemy unit
--- Attack allowed if enemy is within 8-directional attack range
